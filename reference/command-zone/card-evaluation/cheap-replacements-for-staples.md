## Purpose and Overview

  - Covers 20 high-priced Commander staples and offers cheaper cards that replicate all or part of each one's function.
  - Breaks each staple into the roles it fills (trigger doubling, card draw, protection, and so on) and suggests budget options for each role.
  - Gives current prices for both the staples and the replacements.
  - States that players who already own these staples should keep playing them. The episode is aimed at players deciding whether to buy them.
  - Recurring theme: adding redundancy (more cards that do the deck's main thing) can replace explosive doublers or protection pieces and makes a deck more consistent.

## Closing Guidance

  - Keep the expensive staples you already own. Before buying new copies, look for cheaper cards that fit your deck's synergy.
  - Most of these staples are likely to be reprinted, so wait and upgrade later rather than paying peak prices.
  - Prefer redundancy and consistency over explosive doublers or protection.
  - The hosts say recent reprints have not kept card prices down and hope for a dedicated reprint set.
  - Viewers are invited to share their own budget replacements in the comments and to share the episode as a reference guide.
